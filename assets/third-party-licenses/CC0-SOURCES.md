# License records for the retained asset sources

This file records the license basis used for the files in the usable library.
The asset-level provenance and SHA-256 values are in `../LICENSE-LEDGER.csv`.

## Kenney

Selected Kenney asset pages identify the packs as Creative Commons CC0. The
official pages and first-party download URLs are listed in `../SOURCES.md`.

CC0 deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Quaternius

The selected legacy pack pages identify the downloads as CC0 and link to the
author's official Google Drive folders. The pack page, not a filename or a
mirror, is the license evidence. Current Quaternius releases may use QAL;
re-check a specific page before adding new packs.

CC0 deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Poly Haven

Poly Haven's official license is CC0:

https://polyhaven.com/license

The selected maps are 1K derivatives downloaded from the official Poly Haven
CDN listed by its API. No EXR or Blender source is retained here.

## Attribution and modifications

CC0 does not require attribution, but this repository keeps source links for
provenance and auditability. Rescaling, converting, recolouring, rigging,
retargeting, or combining a source with original game art creates a project
derivative; record that change in the feature/asset note when it is imported
into Roblox.
